How to stop paying for the domains & mailboxes

Key point: deleting mailboxes/domains inside Smartlead does NOT stop any billing. Smartlead is only the sending app. The money is charged by the vendors (Aerosend, InboxKit) and the domain registrars. You must stop it at the vendor, not in Smartlead.

Money flows from three places:

Paid toWhat the charge isHow to stop it for good
InboxKit $39/mo plan (renews on the 23rd) + wallet auto-top-up (when credits <10 → auto-buys 300). Mailboxes cost 3 credits/mo each. In the account: disable auto-top-up (kills surprise charges), cancel the subscription, cancel/delete the mailboxes. All doable via the connected API — can be done now on your word.
Aerosend Monthly per mailbox + domains. Account active and paid. Only in their dashboard/chat (app.aerosend.io) — "cancel anytime, no refunds". Their API has no cancel. Message the chat: cancel the subscription and all mailboxes.
"Orphan" Payment to this vendor already failed — which is why the boxes are dying. Nothing to cancel: the domains lapse on their own (May already gone, July frozen, Aug expires Aug 20). If a card is still being charged, block it at the bank.
Reliable backstop against any charge you can't trace: find the card that pays for all this and replace/block it — at the vendors or at the bank. Then nothing can charge, whoever the vendor turns out to be.
